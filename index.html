Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about offline playback, privacy, and how Unwind works.

Does Unwind work without Wi-Fi or mobile data?

Yes. Unwind is built for local audio files already stored on your phone. Playback, playlists, listening stats, A-B loop, and library tools work completely offline. An internet connection is only used if you choose to search public lyrics online or download an optional on-device translation language model.

Does Unwind show ads or require a subscription?

No. Unwind is 100% free with zero ads, no pop-ups, and no subscriptions.

Can Unwind download or stream copyrighted songs?

No. Unwind is strictly a local offline media player for audio files already on your device. It does not download, stream, or distribute music.

Are my music files or listening stats uploaded anywhere?

Never. Your audio files, playlists, and listening statistics stay on your phone inside Android’s app storage. Read the full Privacy Policy for complete details.
